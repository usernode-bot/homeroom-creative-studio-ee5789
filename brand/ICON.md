# App icon

`icon.png` is the group's own artwork (supplied by a member on 2026-10-13),
not the platform's Lucide template: a glossy rainbow paint palette with a
brush and sparkles above the words "Homeroom Creative Studio", on a deep
navy rounded tile. Source: a 1254 x 1254 PNG, resized to 512 x 512 and
quantized to 256 colours to fit the 256 KB icon limit.

`public/app-icon.png` is a 180 x 180 copy of the same image, used as the
browser favicon and apple-touch-icon.
